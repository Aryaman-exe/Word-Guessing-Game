# 🔤 Word Guessing Game

Guess the hidden word before your lives run out — while a mildly disrespectful Python program judges your every decision.

Choose a category, select your difficulty, and uncover the secret word one letter at a time.

## 🎮 How It Works

First, enter your name and choose a difficulty:

- 🟢 **Easy** — 8 lives
- 🟡 **Medium** — 6 lives
- 🔴 **Hard** — 4 lives

Then choose a category:

- 🐼 **Animals**
- 🌎 **Countries**
- 🎮 **Games**

The game randomly selects a word from your chosen category.

Guess the word one letter at a time. Correct letters are revealed, while incorrect guesses cost you a life.

Guess the entire word before your lives reach zero to win.

## ✨ Features

- 🎲 Random word selection
- 🎚️ Three difficulty levels
- 📚 Multiple word categories
- ❤️ Life-based gameplay
- 🔁 Play again option
- ✅ Input validation
- 🚫 Prevents repeated guesses
- ⌨️ Typewriter-style text effects
